Our Origin

A History of Innovation

Founded in 2021, the AI & Innovation Club started with a small group of passionate tech enthusiasts looking to demystify artificial intelligence on campus.

What began as casual weekend study groups discussing neural networks quickly evolved into a university-wide hub for hands-on machine learning, hackathons, and industry partnerships. Today, we stand over 500 members strong, dedicated to exploring the ethical, practical, and futuristic applications of AI.
